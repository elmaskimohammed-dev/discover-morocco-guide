A taste of the 15-point checklist

  • The seller is the registered owner, and their identity matches the title.
  • You have a fresh title certificate showing no mortgage or opposition.
  • The land is not agricultural and is genuinely buildable.
  • Every payment is traceable through the notary or bank.
  • You have an exit plan, including how sale proceeds can leave Morocco.
